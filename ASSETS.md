# Asset sources and licensing

This directory is reserved for game-ready art assets. Do not upload third-party files without checking their license.

## Approved CC0 source packs

| Pack | Source | Intended usage | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kenney Tiny Farm | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm | 16x16 farm tiles | CC0 |
| Kenney Tiny Town | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town | 16x16 town tiles | CC0 |
| Kenney UI Pack - Pixel Adventure | https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-pixel-adventure | Menus and buttons | CC0 |

Kenney's licensing information: https://kenney.nl/support

## Import structure

- `public/assets/tiles/farm/` — Tiny Farm tile images
- `public/assets/tiles/town/` — Tiny Town tile images
- `public/assets/ui/` — Pixel Adventure UI sprites
- `public/assets/characters/` — Custom character sprites (check licenses individually)
- `public/assets/animals/` — Custom or appropriately licensed animals

Keep source ZIP files out of the repository until they are downloaded and inspected. Commit extracted game-ready PNG files with the original license notices where included.

## Not approved for this public repository

Sprout Lands free/basic packs: non-commercial use only, and no redistribution of their source assets. Do not upload them here. See https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack .
